Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about working with me.

How long does a typical project take?

Timeline depends on scope. A simple website takes 1–2 weeks. A full custom web app or e-commerce site can take 4–8 weeks. I'll give you a clear timeline during our first consultation.

What information do you need to start?

I need your project goals, target audience, design preferences (or existing branding), content/copy, and budget range. The more detail you provide, the better I can plan.

Do you offer post-launch support?

Yes! I offer ongoing maintenance, updates, and support packages after launch. Ask me about monthly retainer options for continuous care of your site.

Can you work with clients outside Kenya?

Absolutely. I work with clients globally. All communication is done via email, WhatsApp, or video call. Payments accepted via bank transfer and mobile money.

What is your payment structure?

I typically work with a 50% upfront deposit and 50% on project completion. For larger projects, I can arrange milestone-based payment schedules.

Do you sign NDAs or contracts?

Yes, I'm happy to sign NDAs and work agreements. A project contract is standard for all engagements to protect both parties.
